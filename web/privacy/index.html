Privacy policy

KubeCon Companion is a free event guide for KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026, made by Nevercode Ltd (Codemagic). Last updated November 10, 2026.

The short version

The app has no accounts and no ads. We don't collect, store or sell personal data. Everything you save stays on your device. The web version counts visits and taps anonymously with Plausible (see below); the Android and iPhone apps don't.

Data on your device

  • Saved sessions and reminders are stored only on your device and removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Location, if you allow it, is used on your device to show where you are on the venue map and to give directions. It is never sent to us.
  • Camera, if you allow it, is used only to scan a QR code to import an agenda. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device for sessions you saved.

Sharing

When you choose to share your agenda or your current spot, the app creates a link that contains that information and hands it to the share option you pick. Only the people you send it to see it. We don't receive a copy.

Usage statistics (web only)

On kubecon.codemagic.app we use Plausible Analytics to count page visits and which features are used, such as opening a session, getting directions or tapping an install button. Plausible sets no cookies and stores no personal data or IP addresses; it only reports totals. Search text and locations are never sent. The Android and iPhone apps send no usage statistics.

Network requests

The app downloads the public event schedule from kubecon.codemagic.app. Like any website, the hosting service may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and operations. Links you open to other sites, such as speaker profiles or maps, are covered by those sites' own policies.

Children

The app is meant for conference attendees and is not directed at children.
