KubeCon Companion

Schedule, venue map with turn-by-turn directions, and reminders for KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026.

Android

Get it from Google Play. It's a free open beta, so updates arrive automatically.

Get it on Google Play
  1. Tap the button and sign in to Google if asked.
  2. Tap Become a tester, then the Google Play link, then Install.
Or download the APK directly
APK: about 77 MB · Android 7.0 or newer. Open the file, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.

iPhone

Get the native app through Apple's TestFlight. It's free and takes a minute.

Get the iPhone app
  1. Tap the button, then install TestFlight from the App Store if asked.
  2. Back on the TestFlight page, tap Accept, then Install.
Or use the web app
Web app: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Any device

Use it right in the browser. Nothing to install.

Open kubecon.codemagic.app
Built and shipped with Codemagic. This is a Flutter app, built, tested and released to the web, Android and iOS by Codemagic CI/CD. See the source on GitHub.
